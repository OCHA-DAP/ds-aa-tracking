This site aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA, in line with the United Nations' commitment to accessible digital content. It is built to be used with a keyboard, with a screen reader, at high zoom, and in English, French and Spanish.Ce site vise le niveau AA des Règles pour l’accessibilité des contenus Web (WCAG) 2.1, conformément à l’engagement des Nations Unies en faveur de contenus numériques accessibles. What the site does

  • Every page can be used with the keyboard alone: Tab moves between controls, Enter or Space activates them, Escape closes a panel or goes back to the world map. A 'skip to content' link is the first stop on each page, and the control with focus is outlined.
  • Text keeps a contrast of at least 4.5:1 against its background; the layout works at 200% zoom and on a phone.
  • The language switch in the header (English, French, Spanish) applies to every page and is remembered.
  • The headline figures of each page are given in words, not only in charts; the Map's table lists everything the map shows, with its totals, and downloads as a CSV file. Charts are not described one by one.
  • Nothing flashes. Movement (the map zoom, the panel) is turned off for readers who have asked their system for reduced motion.

Known limits

  • The map itself is a picture. The country names and pins drawn over it are the buttons a keyboard and a screen reader reach, and the table under the map lists the same frameworks.
  • Charts are pictures: their figures are in the text and tables around them, not in the chart.
  • Framework documents and reports are published by their authors as PDF files; their accessibility is theirs.
  • Social media posts on the Media page are shown as they were published on their platforms.

The data another way

The Map's table of frameworks and the historical-activations grid download as CSV files. If a figure or a page is not usable for you, write to us and we will send it in a form that works.
