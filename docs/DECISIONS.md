# Decision log: the AA portfolio tracker

Decisions about this site, the OCHA anticipatory action (AA) portfolio tracker, were taken on recorded calls, in the owner's messages to working sessions, in comment threads on review copies of pages, in pull requests and in code comments. This log brings them together, so that anyone can answer: was this decided, by whom, when and why, and is it still in force? It was filled in on 2026-10-28 from every record available, at the owner's request. It follows the decision-log convention of the team's knowledge base (ds-knowledge-base `docs/DESIGN.md`).

## How to read it

- **Who.** *The owner* is the product owner who rules on the site. *Team call* is one of the recorded AA tracker follow-up calls (4 Aug, 27 Aug, 10 Sep, 21 Sep, 2 Oct, 9 Oct); the 16 Oct recording is one AA team member's review. *Brainstorm* is the 25 Sep session. Other people appear by role only: an AA team member, a CERF reviewer, the learning lead, a regional office reviewer.
- **Sources.** "9 Oct call, 03:06" is a minute mark in that call's Teams recording and transcript (internal; ask the AA team). "Dictated notes" are the owner's notes of the 25 Sep brainstorm and the 1 Oct call. "Owner message" is an instruction the owner gave in a working session; "artifact comment" is a comment the owner left on a review copy of a page. PR numbers and commit hashes are this repository's. "KB D115" is decision D115 in the knowledge base's `docs/DESIGN.md`.
- **Order.** Entries are numbered in date order, D1 onwards, in sections by month. A superseded decision stays in the log, marked **Superseded by** the later one, which is marked **Supersedes**. Where sources disagree, the later owner ruling wins.
- **Status.** *done* (with the PR or commit), *in force* (a rule with nothing to build), *in progress* (built, not yet merged), *not built* or *not checked*.
- **Adding a decision.** Append it with the next number, give it a source a reader can follow, and mark any decision it replaces in both entries. Use roles, never names.

## Decision log


### 2026-08

- **D1 · One tracking database, seeded from the AA team's workbooks** (owner, 2026-08-07; commit 6c344925; README). The portfolio was tracked in several team members' spreadsheets, which disagreed in places. **Decision:** Build one tracking database and review site, seeded from those workbooks. The workbooks are read from a local folder and are not committed to this public repository. **Status:** done.
- **D2 · A framework version is an endorsed document, entered rather than inferred** (owner, 2026-08-24; commits 84b7d8ce of 10 Aug and b7b01ee8; DESIGN.md "Unified version registry"). On 10 Aug versions became the unit that every version-specific fact (envelope, people covered, calendar, activations) attaches to. Versions had until then been inferred from sheet revision dates and knowledge-base pages. **Decision:** A version is a document that was endorsed: by the ERC for a major version (a new commitment or validity), or by the CERF secretariat for a minor one that keeps the validity and budget. From now on versions are entered, not inferred, and the source of each end-of-validity date is recorded (stated in the document, by convention, or inherited). **Status:** done. How versions are labelled: D55.
- **D3 · There are no regional frameworks** (owner, 2026-08-24; commit b7b01ee8). The Dry Corridor framework covers several countries under one document, and the sheets gave it a regional pseudo-code. **Decision:** A multi-country framework is a group of national frameworks that share a document. There is no regional pseudo-country; each country is its own framework. **Status:** done; restated for the trigger timelines in D97.
- **D4 · Funds are OCHA pooled funds only; co-financing is not a fund** (owner, 2026-08-24; commit b7b01ee8; DESIGN.md "Funds as a dimension"). The sheets listed agency co-financing beside CERF and pooled-fund money. **Decision:** The fund list holds CERF, the country-based pooled funds and the regional funds. Agency co-financing is recorded with a free-text financier, never as a fund: it is not OCHA money and there is no allocation record for it. Early-action allocations are whatever CERF tags as early action; the tracker does not redraw that line. **Status:** done. How early action is shown: D37.
- **D5 · Every version has at least one trigger window** (owner, 2026-08-24; commits 193c7f14 and b3365f60; DESIGN.md "Window-first"). Funding and activations hung off versions, which could not express a partial activation or a budget per window. **Decision:** Every version has at least one explicit window, even a single-window framework. Envelopes, people covered and activations attach to a window; a figure a source gives only for the whole version is parked as unattributed until it can be split. **Status:** done 2026-09-21 (commit b3365f60).
- **D6 · How conflicts between the sheets and the knowledge base are settled** (owner, 2026-08-25; commits 4788c1ae, 13d3514b and 25157cbc). Seeding found figures in the sheets that the knowledge base lacked, and a few that disagreed with it. **Decision:** Where a sheet has a figure and the knowledge base is silent, the sheet fills the gap; only conflicting amounts and unverified evidence are flagged for review. Activations inferred from a web sweep that CERF's records contradict are removed (Philippines 2021 and 2022). **Status:** done.
- **D7 · The front page is a map with a few key figures** (team call, 2026-08-27; 27 Aug call, 27:24 and 52:57). The call discussed what the AA page on OCHA's website should show, and asked for an appealing landing page. **Decision:** Lead with a map and key figures: number of frameworks, pre-arranged money, people covered. **Status:** done (landing map from 2026-09-09, commit e10b3356); figures extended in D80.
- **D8 · CERF and the pooled funds in one view** (team call, 2026-08-27; 27 Aug call, 25:33). **Decision:** Show CERF and the country and regional funds in one combined view, broken down by fund, not in parallel views. The owner restated this on 25 Sep: CERF and the pooled funds, not one against the other. **Status:** done; fund toggle added in D38.
- **D9 · Localisation note: CERF also sub-grants to international NGOs** (team call, 2026-08-27; 27 Aug call, 24:42). The localisation footnote implied that CERF sub-grants go only to local actors. **Decision:** Correct the footnote. **Status:** not checked against the current Financing and Activities notes.

### 2026-09

- **D10 · The database is the record, and the entry page writes to it directly** (owner, 2026-09-10; commits 4344bed4, 831e97b4, 09a41c4d and 455a1d1c). The loader rebuilt tables from the workbooks, which would erase anything edited in the database; a first entry path went through a GitHub issue and a knowledge-base workflow. **Decision:** The dev database is the single source of truth; the workbook full refresh is retired and schema changes are additive only. Frameworks are entered on the entry page, which reads a dropped document with an AI extraction (one model), shows a field-by-field difference against the database and saves directly, recording who entered each change. The GitHub issue bridge is retired. **Status:** done. Framework documents now come in only this way: D107.
- **D11 · No chatbot in the first version** (team call, 2026-09-10; 10 Sep call, 55:49; 2 Oct call, 25:01). A chatbot on the site was suggested on several calls. **Decision:** Too much for the first version. On the 2 Oct call it was parked with OCHA IT. **Status:** not built, by decision.
- **D12 · Non-framework allocations and technical support are recorded and shown** (team call, 2026-09-10; 10 Sep call, 50:58–54:43; 21 Sep call, 06:59–09:52 and 22:30–23:51; owner message, 2026-09-28). Some AA money and advice goes to countries without an OCHA framework, and a framework need not carry OCHA money (Palau's is unfunded). **Decision:** Record allocations made without a framework against their country and hazard, and record technical support (no funding commitment) per country and hazard. The owner named Palau and Tonga as technical support only on 28 Sep. **Status:** done (map layers in D19); the list grew in D81.
- **D13 · Three framework statuses: active, being updated, in development** (team call, 2026-09-21; 21 Sep call, 06:59–09:52; DESIGN.md "Window-first"). The first map had two statuses. An AA team member pointed out that a framework under revision is not the same as one in development. **Decision:** A version is endorsed, in development or in pre-development; "superseded" is inferred, and "retired" is a manual flag on the framework. A framework is active (latest version endorsed, valid, not fully triggered), being updated (an endorsed framework that fully triggered, expired or is being revised) or in development (no endorsed version yet). One database view holds the rule and every page reads it. **Status:** done (commits ee58e8c1, 70a3d1e6). The two-tone "being updated" pin (commit b5e19604) was accepted on the 9 Oct call (07:45). Later rulings: D94, D95.
- **D14 · When money is pre-arranged and when it is released** (team call, 2026-09-21; 21 Sep call, 22:30–23:51 and 24:39–30:26; DESIGN.md "Window-first"). **Decision:** Pre-arranged money stays pre-arranged until the framework is retired; a framework being updated keeps its latest envelope. When a window activates, its money counts as released. A pooled-fund allocation made up front but not triggered counts as pre-arranged until an activation draws on it. **Status:** done. Refined by D35, D52 and D59.
- **D15 · A missing sector reads "not yet specified"** (team call, 2026-09-21; 21 Sep call, 11:54). **Decision:** Show a missing sector as "not yet specified", not "null". **Status:** not found in the code on 28 Oct; not checked whether any blank sector still shows.
- **D16 · How donor shares of AA money are attributed** (team call, 2026-09-21; 21 Sep call, 14:39–20:43; DESIGN.md "Donor shares"). Donors report their AA funding each year under the Grand Bargain and ask the team for the figures. **Decision:** A donor's share of a pooled fund's AA money is its share of the fund's income received that year. Contributions earmarked to OCHA's AA work are shown directly. The current year is marked as provisional. **Status:** built on the donor page (commit 41047427); the page left the public site in D78. The call also agreed to attribute a donor's AA top-up to a pooled fund 100% to that donor; the owner has since left that open (see Open questions).

The dev database was losing public network access; only Databricks can reach it. **Decision:** A nightly Databricks job exports the AA tables to a snapshot in blob storage; the GitHub Actions publish restores it into a local Postgres and builds the site from it. The owner preferred this to building the site on Databricks. **Status:** done (PR #1).
